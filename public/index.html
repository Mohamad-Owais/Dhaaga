Questions to Ask an Elder

Spark a Forgotten Memory

Sometimes the most breathtaking stories need just a gentle question to unravel. Read one to them while recording:

🌧️ Childhood • Weather

"Tell me about the heaviest rains you saw as a child. What did the courtyard smell like?"

Prompts vivid sensory details of old tiled roofs, mud scents, and rain snacks.

🍲 Heritage • Food

"Which dish made by your mother or grandmother is never made the same way today?"

Unlocks heirloom spices, slow brass pots, festival rituals, and lost tastes.

🚂 Journey • Youth

"Do you remember the first time you stepped onto a train to leave your hometown?"

Brings forward courage, parting goodbyes, steam engines, and new cities.

The First Frost on the Chinars

"When the autumn turned golden, baba would kindle the kangri with dried apple coals. In the morning, Dal lake wore a veil of steam so thick you could only hear the boatman's oar splashing..."

— Asmat Begum (Nani, 82)
हिन्दी Hindi Old Delhi, 1968

The Copper Kettle of Ballimaran

"Dada bought that heavy copper kettle right after the monsoon. Every morning at five, cardamom and crushed ginger simmered on the stove while the street slowly woke to pigeon wings..."

— Pandit Rameshwar (Dada, 88)
اردو Urdu Lucknow, 1961

The Jasmine Plant in the Courtyard

"Ammi would water the raatrani with water left from boiled chickpeas. At night, when the ceiling fans hummed slow, the whole courtyard smelled so sweet you did not need words to sleep..."

— Farida Khatun (Dadi, 79)
